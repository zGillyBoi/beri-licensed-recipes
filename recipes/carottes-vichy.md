# Carottes Vichy (Glazed Carrots)

Adapted from "Carottes Vichy (Glazed Carrots)" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Carrots simmered in Vichy water, butter, and a whisper of sugar until tender and lacquered — a humble side that shines with sweetness and shine.

Serves about 3 · 25 minutes · Vegetarian · Contains: milk

## Ingredients

- 4 carrots, peeled and sliced into 5 mm rounds (500 g)
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (30 g)
- 2¼ teaspoons caster sugar
- ¾ teaspoon fine salt
- ¾ cup water, sparkling mineral preferred (200 ml)
- A small handful of flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped

## Method

1. Arrange the carrot rounds in a single layer in a wide sauté pan. Add butter, sugar, salt, and just enough water to barely cover the carrots — no more. Bring to a vigorous simmer over medium-high heat, uncovered. Cook, swirling the pan occasionally, until the liquid has almost entirely evaporated and a syrupy glaze remains.
2. Off the heat, scatter the chopped parsley over the glazed carrots. Toss gently to coat, then taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately — the glaze tightens as it cools.
