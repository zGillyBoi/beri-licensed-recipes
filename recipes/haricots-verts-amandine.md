# Haricots Verts Amandine

Adapted from "Haricots Verts Amandine" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Tender-crisp French green beans blanched to vivid green and finished in foamy browned butter scattered with toasted slivered almonds — the essence of classical French elegance.

Serves about 2 · 20 minutes · Vegetarian · Contains: milk, tree nuts

## Ingredients

- 4 cups haricots verts, stem ends trimmed, French green beans (400 g)
- ¼ cup unsalted butter (60 g)
- ⅓ cup slivered almonds (50 g)
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon fine salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground white pepper

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. Season it with salt until it tastes like mild sea water — about 15 g salt per litre. Drop the haricots verts in and cook uncovered for exactly 2–3 minutes: the goal is crisp-tender, never soft. Have a large bowl of ice water ready. Drain the beans and immediately plunge them into the ice bath.
2. In a wide sauté pan over medium heat, melt the butter and add the slivered almonds simultaneously. Swirl the pan frequently. The butter will foam, then the foam will subside as water evaporates. Watch carefully — the milk solids will begin to color to a toasty brown while the almonds toast alongside them. The moment the butter smells of hazelnuts and the almonds are golden, remove from heat.
3. Add the drained and thoroughly dried beans to the pan with the beurre noisette and almonds. Toss over medium-low heat for 1 minute until the beans are coated and reheated. Squeeze the lemon juice directly over the pan and toss once more. Season with white pepper and taste for salt. Serve immediately on a warmed plate.
