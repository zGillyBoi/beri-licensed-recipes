# Toasted Farro Salad with Roasted Vegetables

Adapted from "Toasted Farro Salad with Roasted Vegetables" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Whole farro toasted in a dry pan until nutty and golden, then simmered until pleasantly chewy — tossed warm with caramelized roasted vegetables, fresh herbs, lemon, and good olive oil in the manner of Tuscan cucina povera: simply excellent ingredients treated with respect.

Serves about 8 · 1 hour · Vegan · Contains: celery, gluten, tree nuts, wheat

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups whole farro, not pearled (300 g)
- 3¾ cups water or vegetable stock (900 g)
- 1½ zucchinis, cut into 2 cm pieces (250 g)
- 1⅓ cups cherry tomatoes, halved (200 g)
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges (150 g)
- Half an eggplant, cut into 2 cm cubes (200 g)
- ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil, divided (80 g)
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (30 g)
- 1½ teaspoons lemon zest, 1 lemon
- A handful of fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped (20 g)
- A small handful of fresh mint leaves, torn
- 1¼ teaspoons fine sea salt
- ¾ teaspoon dried chili flakes
- ⅓ cup toasted pine nuts or walnuts (40 g)

## Method

1. Place the farro in a large dry skillet over medium heat. Toast, stirring constantly, for 4–6 minutes until the grains smell nutty and are golden — some grains may lightly pop. This step is optional but transforms the flavor dramatically. The toasted grains will have a nuttier, more complex flavor after cooking.
2. Transfer toasted farro to a pot. Cover with generously salted water or stock (3 parts liquid to 1 part farro by volume). Bring to a boil, reduce to a steady simmer, and cook uncovered for 30–40 minutes for whole farro (20–25 for semi-pearled) until the grains are tender but still have a distinct chew — al dente, like a well-cooked barley. Drain any excess liquid.
3. Toss all the vegetables with a generous amount of olive oil, salt, and chili flakes on a large sheet pan. Spread in a single layer — do not crowd or they will steam. Roast at 220 C (425 F) for 25–30 minutes, turning once halfway, until caramelized and charred at the edges — particularly the cherry tomatoes, which should burst and concentrate.
4. While both the farro and vegetables are still warm, combine them in a large bowl. Drizzle with remaining olive oil and lemon juice. Add lemon zest, chili flakes, parsley, mint, and toasted nuts. Toss together gently — you want the warm farro to absorb the lemon and oil. Taste and adjust salt, acid, and oil. The salad is excellent warm, but also very good at room temperature.
