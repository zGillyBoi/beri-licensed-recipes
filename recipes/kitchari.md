# Kitchari

Adapted from "Kitchari" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The Ayurvedic healing porridge of India — split mung dal and basmati rice cooked together with golden ghee, cumin, and turmeric until they dissolve into a warmly fragrant, deeply nourishing, easily digestible whole that is more than the sum of its parts.

Serves about 2 · 45 minutes · Vegetarian · Contains: milk

## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati rice, rinsed (200 g)
- ¾ cup split yellow mung dal, moong dal (150 g)
- 3 tablespoons ghee (40 g)
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 2 teaspoons ground turmeric
- 1¼ teaspoons fine sea salt
- 5 cups water (1200 ml)
- Fresh coriander, lemon, ghee to serve

## Method

1. Melt the ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and fry for 60-90 seconds, stirring, until they begin to pop and turn a shade darker. Add the turmeric and stir for 20 seconds. The spices are bloomed in fat to release their fat-soluble flavour compounds — this step builds the backbone of the dish.
2. Add the rinsed rice and mung dal to the spiced ghee and stir to coat in the turmeric-gold fat. Pour in the water, add salt, and bring to a boil. Reduce to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook for 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the rice and dal have completely merged into a soft, porridge-like consistency. The kitchari should flow slowly from the spoon, not hold its shape.
