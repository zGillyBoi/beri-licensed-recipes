# Southern Succotash

Adapted from "Southern Succotash" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

A summer jubilee of sweet corn, lima beans, and cherry tomatoes cooked in bacon fat — humble vegetables made rich and textured, finished with butter and herbs.

Serves about 2 · 25 minutes · Contains: milk

## Ingredients

- 2¾ cups fresh sweet corn kernels, from about 4 ears (400 g)
- 2 cups baby lima beans, fresh or frozen (300 g)
- 1⅓ cups cherry tomatoes, halved (200 g)
- ⅔ cup streaky bacon, sliced into lardons (100 g)
- ¾ white onion, finely diced (120 g)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (30 g)
- 3 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- A small handful of fresh basil, torn
- ¾ teaspoon fine salt
- ¾ teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Place bacon lardons in a cold, wide skillet or cast-iron pan. Set over medium heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until the fat has rendered completely and the bacon is deeply browned and crisp — about 8–10 minutes. Scoop the bacon out with a slotted spoon onto paper towels. Leave the rendered fat in the pan.
2. In the same pan with the rendered bacon fat, add the onion and cook over medium heat until softened and just beginning to turn golden, about 5 minutes. Add the corn kernels and lima beans along with the thyme. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the corn is tender and starting to blister in patches and the lima beans are heated through and creamy — about 6–8 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and halved cherry tomatoes to the pan. Cook just 2 minutes — enough for the tomato skins to just begin to blister and split without losing their shape. Remove from heat. Add the cold butter and swirl the pan to melt and emulsify it into the vegetables. Season generously with salt and pepper. Scatter the torn basil and crumble the reserved crisp bacon over the top. Serve immediately.
