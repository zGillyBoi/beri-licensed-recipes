# Farro Salad

Adapted from "Farro Salad" by ForkRecipe Kitchen, from the ForkRecipe dataset.
This adaptation is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

An Italian grain salad built on the nutty, chewy foundation of whole farro — tossed still warm with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, peppery arugula, sharp pecorino, and a lemon-olive oil dressing that soaks into every grain.

Serves about 4 · 45 minutes · Vegetarian · Contains: gluten, milk, wheat

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups whole farro, semi-pearled (300 g)
- 1¾ cups cherry tomatoes, halved (250 g)
- 1⅔ cups cucumber, diced, seeds removed (200 g)
- 3 handfuls of wild rocket, arugula (80 g)
- ⅔ cup pecorino Romano, shaved (60 g)
- ¼ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (60 ml)
- ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil (80 ml)
- 1¼ teaspoons fine sea salt and black pepper
- A handful of fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped (20 g)

## Method

1. Rinse the farro. Cook in a large pot of generously salted boiling water for 25-35 minutes (semi-pearled farro) until the grains are tender but still have a firm, chewy bite — think al dente pasta, not soft porridge. Drain and spread on a large tray to cool and release steam. Season immediately with salt and a drizzle of olive oil while still hot so the grains absorb flavour.
2. While the farro is still warm (not hot), combine it in a large bowl with the cherry tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, and rocket. Whisk together the lemon juice and olive oil with a pinch of salt and pour over the salad. Toss well. Scatter shaved pecorino over the top. Taste and adjust — the salad should be bright and lemony, with the farro providing a warm, nutty backbone.
